    Is Mt. Gwanak a Good Mountain to Hike During a Seoul Trip?

    If you're in Seoul and feel a little bored with shopping at Olive Young, Mt. Gwanak could be a good choice to make your trip healthier and more memorable.

    Like many other mountains in Seoul, it's easy to reach by subway. One of the most interesting things about this mountain is the belief that it can help make your wishes come true.

    Some people describe Mt. Gwanak as a mountain with strong energy that encourages people to pursue their goals such as finding a job, passing an exam, or entering their preferred school.

    Starting Point: Gwacheon Station

    1. Take Subway Line 4 and get off at Gwacheon Station.
    2. Find Exit 7 and walk straight.
    3. Turn left at the bridge and continue uphill.
    4. Cross toward Gwacheon HyangGuo.
    5. Follow Trail 2 toward Mt. Gwanak.
    6. After approximately 40 minutes you'll reach Yeonjuam Temple.
    7. Enjoy the view and rest if necessary.
    8. Continue uphill toward the summit.

    A Place That Inspires Visitors to Pursue Their Goals

    According to local stories, reaching the summit is not enough. You must visit a prayer area located near the top of the mountain.

    1. Climb onto the large rock carefully.
    2. Locate the narrow path beside the rock.
    3. Walk down a small staircase.
    4. You'll find a prayer area with a Buddha statue.
    5. Take a moment to pray or reflect.
    Please be respectful. This is a place where visitors come to pray and reflect quietly.

    Believe it or notow Before Hiking

    1. Hiking time varies depending on fitness level.
    2. It took me around 2.5 hours to reach the summit.
    3. Wear sturdy hiking shoes.
    4. Bring enough water.
    5. You can buy snacks and cup noodles at Yeonjuam Temple.
    6. Weekends can be crowded.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How can I get to Mt. Gwanak by public transport?
    Take Seoul Subway Line 4 and get off at Gwacheon Station.
    Is there only one hiking route?
    No. Several routes lead to the summit.
    How is Mt. Gwanak connected to wish-making?
    Many visitors believe that a prayer area near the summit is associated with making wishes and pursuing personal goals.
    Can beginners hike Mt. Gwanak?
    Yes, but some sections can be steep and rocky.
