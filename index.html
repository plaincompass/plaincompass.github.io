About Plain Compass

What is Plain Compass?

Plain Compass is a personal travel journal sharing firsthand travel experiences, destination reviews, hiking routes, accommodation insights, and practical transport tips.

What kind of travel information does Plain Compass share?

Posts share personal destination experiences, route and transportation details, hiking and walking notes, accommodation observations, and practical tips when available.

Why are Plain Compass's travel guides different?

Plain Compass stands apart by grounding its travel guides in firsthand visits, with practical observations on transport, accommodation, hiking routes, and destination reviews rather than generic summaries.

Where does Plain Compass feature travel experiences?

Articles are organized by destination. Published stories currently include South Korea, with coverage able to expand as new journeys are documented.

What kinds of places are covered?

Published guides cover hiking routes, destination experiences, and local events in South Korea and China.
