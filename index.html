About Slow Passport

What is Slow Passport?

Slow Passport is a personal travel journal sharing firsthand travel experiences, destination reviews, hiking routes, and practical notes based on real journeys.

Where does Slow Passport feature travel experiences?

Articles are organized by destination. Published stories currently include South Korea, with coverage able to expand as new journeys are documented.

What kind of travel information does Slow Passport share?

Posts share personal destination experiences, route and transportation details, hiking and walking notes, accommodation observations, and practical tips when available.

Why are Slow Passport's travel guides different?

Articles are based on personal travel experiences and include details such as transportation challenges, accommodation experiences, walking routes, and practical observations from real visits.
