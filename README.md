# Slow Passport

Exploring the world through trails, local stories, and unforgettable journeys.

Slow Passport is a personal travel blog sharing hiking adventures, local discoveries, transportation tips, accommodation reviews, and independent travel experiences from Korea and beyond.

## About

Slow Passport focuses on real travel experiences and practical information gathered during independent trips.

Topics include:

- Hiking guides
- Travel itineraries
- Public transportation tips
- Accommodation reviews
- Local food experiences
- Hidden destinations
- Travel planning notes
- Honest travel stories

The goal is simple:

To help travelers discover useful information through firsthand experiences rather than generic tourist recommendations.
