Is Mt. Gwanak a Good Mountain to Hike During a Seoul Trip?

A personal hiking guide to Mt. Gwanak from Gwacheon Station, including the route to Yeonjuam Temple, trail tips, and a quiet prayer spot near the summit.

Gwacheon, Seoul, South Korea

  • #MtGwanak
  • #SeoulHiking
  • #KoreaTravel
  • #Gwacheon

If you’re in Seoul and feel a little bored with shopping at Olive Young, Mt. Gwanak could be a good choice to make your trip healthier and more memorable. Like many other mountains in Seoul, it’s easy to reach by subway.

One of the most interesting things about this mountain is the belief that it can help make your wishes come true. I heard a Korean fortune teller say on YouTube that Mt. Gwanak has good energy. I’ve also come across online comments describing it as a mountain with “fire energy,” which is said to encourage people to pursue their goals, such as finding a job, getting into their preferred school, or passing an exam.

One version of the story says that simply climbing the mountain isn’t enough to make your wishes come true. You also have to visit a special place near the summit.

I’ll tell you about that place in this post, so just follow the route I took.

Starting Point: Gwacheon Station

  1. Take Subway Line 4 and get off at Gwacheon Station. Find Exit 7.
  2. When you exit, you’ll see a large road. Go straight.
  3. When you reach the bridge, turn left and continue uphill until Gwacheon Middle School appears.
  4. In front of the middle school, cross the road and go uphill toward Gwacheon HyangGuo.
  5. This is the starting point for Trail 2 on Mt. Gwanak.
  6. From there, follow the road. There aren’t any other specific roads, so choose the widest one.
  7. After walking uphill for about 40 minutes, you’ll see Yeonjuam Temple. It’s easy to find; the trail just continues uphill. When you reach the temple, you can buy noodles or ice cream to recharge.
  8. Although you’re still partway up the mountain, the view is great. Don’t rush to reach the top; take a moment to enjoy it.
  9. After that, there’s a path uphill behind the shop. It gets narrower than the path to the temple, so be careful, as there are many hikers.
  10. After climbing what feels like a million stairs, you’ll find a large rock.
  11. This is an uphill section, so wait your turn if you want to take a picture.

A Place That Inspires Visitors to Pursue Their Goals

Unfortunately, one version of the story says that simply reaching the top isn’t enough to make your wishes come true. You also have to find the place to pray.

Here’s how to get from the top to the prayer place:

  1. You have to climb up onto the rock. This is the most dangerous part of this route, so be extra careful.
  2. Once you’re on the rock (there’s a narrow path beside the rocks), you’ll find a small set of steps leading down.
  3. Go down those steps, and you’ll find a small prayer area with a Buddha statue. This is the place where people make their wishes.
  4. Say a prayer for what you want, and take a deep breath.

Before you visit, keep in mind that this is a place for prayer, so please be quiet. Don’t talk or take photos with sound or flash.

Believe it or not, I prayed at this place, and about a month later, I got a job at a company I was really excited to join. I can’t say that the prayer caused it, but the timing made the experience feel special to me.

What to Know Before Hiking

  1. The website estimates that the ascent takes two hours, but the actual time depends on your fitness and pace. It took me two and a half hours to reach the top.
  2. Parts of the trail are rocky, so wear sturdy hiking shoes.
  3. Trekking poles are helpful but not essential.
  4. Bring water.
  5. About halfway up, you’ll find Yeonjuam Temple. There are public restrooms, and you can buy cup noodles and ice cream there. The vendors accept cash only, so bring some. Cup noodles cost around 3,000 won, and ice cream costs around 2,000 won.
  6. The trail can be crowded, so watch your step and be considerate of other hikers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I get to Mt. Gwanak by public transport?

You can get there by subway. One convenient option is to take Line 4 to Gwacheon Station.

Is there only one way to hike up the mountain?

No, there are several routes. The trail from Gwacheon Station is one of the easiest and quickest options.

How is Mt. Gwanak connected to making wishes come true?

Some people describe the mountain as having “fire energy” and visit a prayer place near the summit. This is a local belief, not a guarantee that wishes will come true.

Can it really help make your wishes come true?

I believe the experience felt meaningful to me. I got a new job about a month after I prayed, although I can’t say that the prayer caused it.

Is hiking easy?

I didn’t find it very easy. If you’re not used to exercising, you may find it difficult.
