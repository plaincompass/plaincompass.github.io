Why I Visited

I had just learned that AeroK had opened a direct flight from Seoul to Obihiro, so I booked the first one available. I’d already been to Sapporo and was looking for another small city in Hokkaido that didn’t feel over‑touristed. The direct flight made the trip convenient, even though it turns out to be seasonal.

What Stood Out

The first thing that struck me was the vast white plains unfolding before me as I took the shuttle from Obihiro Airport to Obihiro Station—an image that felt liberating. Near Obihiro Station, I stayed at Comfort Hotel Obihiro and walked to Tokachigawasuikei Ryokuchi Park, where I met a red squirrel. This encounter left a deep impression on me and made me think of Obihiro as a nature-friendly city. The park was a magnificent sight with the plains covered in white and trees scattered around, giving off a Nordic vibe.

The food was a highlight. The butadon inside Obihiro Station was the best I’ve tasted in Japan; the owner kept grilling pork over charcoal right in front of us. Even the supermarket dango and takoyaki were tasty. I also tried Sapporo Classic, which is only sold within Hokkaido. While staying at Comfort Hotel Obihiro, I walked to the park and even met a red squirrel, adding a small, delightful moment to the walk.

Practical Notes

  • Flight from Seoul to Obihiro: 3 hours.
  • After landing, I took a shuttle from Obihiro Airport to Obihiro Station for 1,000 yen.
  • I arrived at 4 PM on the first day and departed at 10 AM on the last day.
  • I stayed at Comfort Hotel Obihiro (13 Chome‑2 Nishi 1 Jominami, Obihiro, Hokkaido 080‑0011).
  • The roads were slightly slippery, so I brought crampons.
  • The weather was clear; no waiting times at any point.
  • Cards were accepted at almost all places.
  • The station itself was crowded, but the rest of the city was easy to access.
  • The walking paths required caution because of the slipperiness.
  • I recommend this spot for people who have moved on from Sapporo or find Biei too crowded, but it may not satisfy those looking for Biei‑level snowfall.

Final Thoughts

Walking slowly through Tokachigawasuikei Ryokuchi Park, feeling the serene environment and seeing the first footprints in the white snow, was soothing for my mind. The photos I took—snow‑covered plains with exotic trees—looked beautiful. Above all, the food here, especially butadon, was the most delicious I’ve experienced in Japan, and I can see why Obihiro is called the birthplace of this dish. The direct flight is seasonal, but the overall experience was worthwhile.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the flight from Seoul to Obihiro take?

The flight lasts about 3 hours.

What transportation is available from Obihiro Airport to the city?

A shuttle takes passengers from Obihiro Airport to Obihiro Station for 1,000 yen.

Are credit cards accepted in Obihiro?

Yes, almost all places in Obihiro accept cards.

How slippery are the roads in Obihiro and what should I bring?

The roads are slightly slippery, so it is recommended to bring crampons.

Is walking in Tokachigawasuikei Ryokuchi Park suitable for someone with low fitness?

The walking paths are suitable for those with almost no fitness, but caution is needed because of slipperiness.

What is the must‑try food in Obihiro?

The butadon, grilled pork over charcoal, is considered the best in Japan and is a highlight of the local cuisine.
