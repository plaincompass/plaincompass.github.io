Obihiro Travel Hub

Location: Obihiro, Hokkaido, Japan

Region Overview

Obihiro is a city in Hokkaido, Japan, serving as a gateway to the region's snowy plains and mountainous landscapes. The area is known for its hot spring facilities, red squirrel encounters, and winter activities. Key destinations accessible from Obihiro include Nukabira Lake and Tokachikawa Onsen.

Nukabira Lake is located in the Obihiro area and is accessible via a direct city bus from Obihiro Station. Tokachikawa Onsen is a hot spring facility situated in the Obihiro region; however, it requires bus transfers and has more limited public transportation options compared to the city center. Within Obihiro city, visitors can explore Tokachigawasuikei Ryokuchi Park and enjoy local dining options, including butadon.

Places Covered

Nukabira Lake

Nukabira Lake is a frozen lake located in Hokkaido, Japan. The site features a walk-around path that allows visitors to view the surrounding landscape. During the visit, the snow was thinner than expected, and ice cleats were required due to melting ice conditions. From the back of the lake, Mount Asahi is visible. Travelers typically reach Nukabira Lake via a bus ride from Obihiro.

Tokachikawa Onsen

Tokachikawa Onsen is a hot spring facility in Hokkaido, Japan, distinguished by water that emerges from moss. The facility includes open-air baths for soaking. Access to Tokachikawa Onsen is more complex than other local sites, as public transportation is limited to cash-only buses that require transfers. There are no nearby convenience stores or supermarkets, so visitors should plan accordingly.

Obihiro

Obihiro is a city in Hokkaido, Japan, characterized by snowy plains and mountains. Common activities in the city include walking in Tokachigawasuikei Ryokuchi Park, eating butadon, dango, and takoyaki, and staying at Comfort Hotel Obihiro. The city is well-connected, with a direct flight route from Seoul to Obihiro and a shuttle service from Obihiro Airport to Obihiro Station. Visitors are advised to bring crampons for slippery roads, though card payments are accepted at most places in the city.

Transportation Summary

Travelers can fly directly from Seoul to Obihiro. Upon arrival, a shuttle operates from Obihiro Airport to Obihiro Station, where card payments are accepted. For local travel, a city bus connects Obihiro Station to Nukabira Lake, and card payments are accepted for bus fares within Obihiro city. In contrast, travel to Tokachikawa Onsen requires city buses with one transfer, and these services are cash-only. A shuttle also connects Tokachikawa Onsen to Obihiro Station, but it operates on a cash-only basis.
