Obihiro Travel Hub

Location: Obihiro

Region Overview

Obihiro serves as the base for visits to Nukabira Lake and Tokachikawa Onsen. Nukabira Lake is a frozen lake where a walk on the ice was described. Tokachikawa Onsen is a hot spring location featuring water that emerges from moss.

Nukabira Lake is accessible via a bus ride from Obihiro. Mount Asahi became visible after leaving the lakeside area and moving toward the nearby town. Tokachikawa Onsen is located in a small town with limited public transportation options, including cash-only buses that require transfers.

Places Covered

Nukabira Lake

Nukabira Lake is a frozen lake in Hokkaido. The snow was thinner than expected during the visit in February 2026.

A walk around Nukabira Lake was described. The ice was melting and the snow was thinner than expected during the visit. A wild deer was encountered. The lake itself did not provide views of Mount Asahi. Mount Asahi became visible after leaving the lakeside area and moving toward the nearby town. A bus ride from Obihiro was used to access the area.

Tokachikawa Onsen

Tokachikawa Onsen is a hot spring location in Hokkaido. The area lacks basic infrastructure such as nearby convenience stores and supermarkets.

Visitors soaked in open-air baths. The hot spring water emerges from moss. Public transportation is limited to cash-only buses requiring transfers.

Transportation Summary

A bus ride from Obihiro to Nukabira Lake is available. Card payment was accepted at most places for this route. Public transportation to Tokachikawa Onsen is limited to cash-only buses requiring transfers.
