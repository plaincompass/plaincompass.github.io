Nukabira Lake Winter Walk: Quiet Snowy Landscape in Hokkaido

A peaceful stroll around Nukabira Lake in February 2026, capturing clear skies, Mount Asahi in view, and a surprise wild deer encounter. The ice was melting and the snow thinner than expected, but the easy path and accessible bus ride from Obihiro made it a gentle, Nordic‑style winter forest experience.

Who might find this useful?

Good for

People who love quiet, snowy walks and want to capture the quiet beauty of a frozen lake in Hokkaido.

Less useful for

Those who need a bustling tourist spot, or who prefer a day full of activities rather than a gentle stroll.

Pros and cons

Pros

The scenery was consistently beautiful, even on the walk‑through portions of the lake. The weather was clear and the view of Mount Asahi from the back added a dramatic touch. The path was easy to navigate, and the bus ride from Obihiro was straightforward. I could pay with a card at most places, which was convenient.

Cons

The ice was melting, so the surface was slick and I had to bring ice cleats. The snow was less than expected, which left the lake looking a bit bare.

My Reason for Visiting

I wanted to feel the quiet, snowy landscape of Nukabira Lake. The idea was to experience the calm of a frozen lake and see how the snow blankets the scenery.

What I Liked

Every part of the walk felt picturesque. Even on a simple stroll, the view was lovely. The clear weather meant I could see Mount Asahi in the distance, adding a striking backdrop to the whole scene. The lake itself looked peaceful, and the light on the ice made for great photo opportunities.

What I Didn’t Like

Because the weather was so clear, the ice melted and the snow thinned. I expected a thicker blanket of snow, but it was less than I had imagined. The slick surface made the walk a little risky, and I had to use ice cleats to keep my footing.

A Memorable Moment

On the way to the lake in the morning, I ran into a wild deer. It was both fascinating and a little scary. It reminded me that the area still has a touch of wilderness.

Conditions

The day was clear, but the ice was melting, so the path was slippery. I had to bring ice cleats, a hot pack, and a warm coat to stay comfortable.

Expectations vs Reality

I had imagined a thicker layer of snow covering the lake, but the reality was that the snow was thinner. The clear sky made the ice melt faster than I expected.

Trip Duration

I spent one night and two days at the lake. The main activity was the walk around the lake, which took 20 minutes.

Getting There

I took a city bus from Obihiro Station to the lake. The bus ride was easy. From Obihiro terminal to Nukabira, there is a ticket booth where card is accepted. From Nukabira to Obihiro, card is not accepted.

Accessibility

The path was very easy to access. There were no major obstacles, and the walk was straightforward.

Crowd Level

There were almost no other people around, so the lake felt very quiet and private.

Photo Opportunities

Every part of the walk into Nukabira Lake was a great spot for photos. The clear sky and the distant view of Asahi made the shots especially nice.

Language Barriers

I knew a little Japanese, but I found the bus transfer a bit inconvenient because of the language barrier.

Payment Methods

From Obihiro terminal to Nukabira, there is a ticket booth where card is accepted. From Nukabira to Obihiro, card is not accepted.

Waiting Time

There was no waiting time; the bus and the walk were smooth.

Fitness Level

I have almost no fitness, but the walk was gentle and easy to handle. However, the path was kind of flipped, so I needed ice cleats (아이젠).

Walking Time

The walk around Nukabira Lake took 20 minutes.

Will I Go Again?

I would visit again. The quiet, Nordic‑style winter forest feels unique, and the scenery is still worth seeing.

What to Bring

Ice cleats (the path was very slippery), a hot pack, and a warm coat. These items helped keep me comfortable and safe.

Final thoughts

It wasn’t the dramatic, snow‑filled panorama I had imagined, but the quiet, Nordic‑style winter forest made up for it. The lake’s gentle walk, clear skies, and the distant view of Asahi created a calm and memorable experience. If you’re looking for a peaceful walk with beautiful scenery, Nukabira Lake is a solid choice.
