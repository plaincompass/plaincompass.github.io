Picnic at Shiba Park: Kaisendon and Tokyo Tower Views

A relaxed 3-hour picnic in Shiba Park's shaded lower grounds. Enjoyed Kaisendon while watching Tokyo Tower rise above full-bloom cherry blossoms.

Shiba Park, Tokyo

Public transport access
★★★★★ Excellent (5/5)
  • #Tokyo
  • #TokyoTower
  • #Picnic
  • #CherryBlossoms
  • #ShibaPark

I spent a quiet afternoon at Shiba Prince Park, just ten minutes’ walk from the Tokyo Grand Hotel. After a quick lunch of Kaisendon—waiting about ten minutes at the shop and paying with a card—I spread a mat on the shaded lower grounds and ate while watching Tokyo Tower rise above the cherry blossoms.

The view was simple but satisfying: the tower framed the sky, and the blossoms added a touch of spring. I took several photos, all with the tower in the background, and the whole experience felt relaxed and enjoyable.

Why I Visited

I wanted to see Tokyo Tower and have a picnic. The plan was simple: a short walk from the Tokyo Grand Hotel, a quick lunch, and a few hours to enjoy the view.

What Stood Out

The Kaisendon I bought from a nearby lunch shop was the highlight. The shop is popular with office workers, so I waited about ten minutes before buying. After paying with a card, I walked about ten minutes to the park, spread a mat, and ate while looking at Tokyo Tower. The cherry blossoms were in full bloom, creating an exotic atmosphere with the landmark in the background. The lower part of Prince Shiba Park offered shade and was less crowded than the upper area near the tower, which was packed with tourists and had no shade.

Practical Notes

  • Transport: 10‑minute walk from the hotel to the park.
  • Waiting time: About 10 minutes at the lunch shop.
  • Duration: 3 hours total.
  • Crowd: Quite many, but peaceful.
  • Weather: Very clear, locals mainly picnicked.
  • Payment: The lunch shop accepted cards.
  • Bring: A mat and bottled water.
  • Difficulty: 3 (moderate).
  • Photo spots: Shiba Prince Park is a photo zone wherever you look, with Tokyo Tower in the background.

Final Thoughts

Overall, it was a very satisfying picnic. Eating Kaisendon while seeing Tokyo Tower, a landmark of Tokyo, was excellent. The minced tuna was especially delicious. We took many photos with Tokyo Tower as a backdrop, and all the photos turned out well. If anyone finds a less crowded spot in Shiba Prince Park, taking photos with Tokyo Tower as a backdrop will look pretty.
