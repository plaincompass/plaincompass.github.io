Tokyo Travel Hub

Location: Tokyo

Region Overview

Tokyo includes Akihabara, Lake Kawaguchiko, and Shiba Park. Akihabara is located in Tokyo, while Lake Kawaguchiko is in Yamanashi Prefecture and Shiba Park is in Tokyo.

Akihabara is accessible via subway from a hotel near Tokyo Station, with a 20-minute walk involved. Lake Kawaguchiko is reached by highway bus from Tokyo Station. Shiba Park is a ten-minute walk from the Tokyo Grand Hotel.

Places Covered

Akihabara

Akihabara is a district in Tokyo known for gaming and anime merchandise.

The visitor searched for a PlayStation Portal at Sony’s headquarters and a second-hand store. The main street was packed with people. The visitor walked 20 minutes from a hotel near Tokyo Station using the subway and then walking. The visitor ate crepes and takoyaki near Akihabara Bic Camera.

Lake Kawaguchiko

Lake Kawaguchiko is a lake in Yamanashi Prefecture with views of Mt. Fuji.

The visitor rented an electric bike from 81cafe for 3,200 yen for a full-day rental. The ride lasted about five hours. The visitor stopped at several spots and had a convenience-store lunch in the lake park. The visitor took a highway bus from Tokyo Station, arriving at the lake terminal at 9 am. The shop opened at 9:30 am. The terrain had steep parts.

Shiba Park

Shiba Park is a park in Tokyo located near Tokyo Tower.

The visitor spread a mat on the shaded lower grounds. The visitor bought Kaisendon from a nearby lunch shop, waiting about ten minutes and paying with a card. The visitor walked about ten minutes from the shop to the park. The cherry blossoms were in full bloom. The lower part of the park offered shade and was less crowded than the upper area near the tower.
