Beijing Travel Hub

Location: Beijing

Region Overview

Beijing contains the Forbidden City, Huanghua Qingcheng, Summer Palace, and Temple of Heaven.

The Forbidden City, Summer Palace, and Temple of Heaven are located in central Beijing. Huanghua Qingcheng is located in Huairou District on the outskirts of Beijing and is commonly visited as a separate day trip.

The Forbidden City is an enormous palace complex with relatively few trees. Huanghua Qingcheng features a unique section of the Great Wall that is partially submerged in water. The Summer Palace is a spacious imperial garden centered around a large artificial lake. The Temple of Heaven is a historic park that contains the Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests.

Places Covered

Forbidden City

The Forbidden City is a palace complex in Beijing, China, known for its immense scale and extensive palace architecture.

Visitors arrived by subway and encountered a 30-minute wait at the entrance. The Imperial Garden near the north gate was visited, and reflections of the palace walls could be seen in the surrounding moat. Bag inspections were conducted before entry. Tickets were reserved in advance through the Forbidden City mini-program on WeChat for 60 yuan. Alipay and WeChat Pay were accepted for payment.

Huanghua Qingcheng

Huanghua Qingcheng is a scenic section of the Great Wall in Huairou District, Beijing, known for portions of the wall extending into the reservoir.

The site was visited as part of a separate day tour from Beijing. The visit lasted approximately five hours. A ten-minute walk led to the dock, followed by a five-minute boat ride included in the tour. A lift train was used to reach higher sections of the site. Cable car tickets were purchased in advance through the guide. Payments were made using Alipay or WeChat Pay.

Summer Palace

The Summer Palace is a Qing dynasty imperial garden in Beijing, China.

A subway ride to Beigongmen Station was followed by a short walk to the entrance. The journey took about an hour. Admission cost 30 yuan and was paid through Alipay. Highlights included the Tower of Buddhist Incense, floating cruise boats on Kunming Lake, and winding garden waterways.

Temple of Heaven

The Temple of Heaven is a historic religious complex in Beijing, China, where Ming and Qing emperors performed ceremonies dedicated to heaven.

A 30-minute subway ride from the hotel was followed by a walk to Temple of Heaven Park. The Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests was visited. Large crowds were present throughout the site, including many visitors wearing elaborate traditional costumes.

Transportation Summary

The subway was used to reach the Forbidden City, Summer Palace, and Temple of Heaven within central Beijing.

The journey to the Temple of Heaven involved a 30-minute subway ride followed by a short walk. Reaching the Summer Palace required a subway ride to Beigongmen Station and a walk to the entrance.

Huanghua Qingcheng was visited as a separate day trip from Beijing. Transportation was arranged through a guided tour, followed by a short walk to the dock, a boat ride, and a lift train to reach higher sections of the Great Wall.
