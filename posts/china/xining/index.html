Xining, China: Rocky Mountains and Dry Late-August Weather

3N/4D trip to Xining for Qinghai Lake. Dry, cool late-August weather with stunning rocky mountain views. Taxi transfer cost 120 yuan. Use Alipay/WeChat Pay.

Xining, Qinghai, China

I flew from Seoul to Xining over the course of three nights and four days, with the main goal of visiting Qinghai Lake. The itinerary began with a flight to Qingdao, where I spent an eight‑hour layover, followed by a three‑hour flight to Xining. I had booked a pickup through Trip.com for my arrival, but the driver details never arrived, so I ended up taking a taxi for 120 yuan. Trip.com promptly refunded the pre‑booked fare and covered the taxi cost.

The journey was defined by the striking rocky mountain scenery and the pleasantly dry late‑August weather. Navigating the crowded markets and dealing with limited English were challenges, but the food and views made the trip worthwhile for anyone interested in the region.

Why I Visited

My primary destination was Chaka Salt Lake, a high‑altitude lake near Qinghai Lake. Before the trip, I had little information about the area, which made me anxious. Compared to major hubs like Shanghai or Beijing, Xining is a domestic‑only destination and not a typical tourist hotspot, so I wanted to avoid the uncertainties of bus travel. Pre‑booking a pickup through Trip.com was intended to ensure a smooth arrival.

What Stood Out

The landscape was the most striking part of the experience. Even before landing, the view from the plane showed vast rocky mountains. When I exited the taxi near Xining Station, the screen‑like backdrop of mountains unfolded around me, creating a dramatic frame for the station and the surrounding city. This scenery made for great photos.

The weather in late August was surprisingly pleasant—dry, clear, and cool in the shade, with no humidity. The air quality and climate felt far from a typical hot August day.

I stayed at the Qinghai Xiadu Hotel for one night in Xining, one night near Qinghai Lake, and the final night back at the Qinghai Xiadu Hotel, completing a 3‑night, 4‑day itinerary.

A memorable moment was visiting the residential areas and markets east of Xining Station. The market was very crowded, making navigation difficult, but the presence of many Tibetan people in the downtown area—especially in the market and shopping malls—added to the local flavor.

The food was decent. I enjoyed Jianbing and a Tibetan yak stew that was delicious.

Practical Notes

Transportation

  • Flight Route: Seoul → Qingdao (2 h), 8‑hour layover, Qingdao → Xining (3 h).
  • Layover: The eight‑hour layover in Qingdao was manageable. A hotel inside the airport makes it convenient.
  • Airport Transfer: I pre‑booked a pickup via Trip.com but encountered an issue with the driver’s location. After contacting Trip.com internationally, they offered a full refund and covered the taxi fare. I took a taxi from Xining Airport to the city center, which cost 120 yuan.

Language & Communication

English is the least understood language in this region compared to other places. However, people were kind and did their best to help. Airport staff guided us well during the transfer. Using translation apps is advisable.

Payment

Alipay and WeChat Pay are the primary payment methods. The Alipay transit card can also be used for bus tickets.

Health & Fitness

I was concerned about altitude in the Xining area, but it was better than expected. The only physical difficulty was a bit of trouble climbing the hill at Ta’ers Monastery. Walking on the flat terrain in downtown Xining (elevation 2300 m) or at Chaka Salt Lake (elevation 3200 m) was fine.

Crowds

Downtown Xining had many people. The market and shopping malls were especially busy with locals, making movement difficult due to crowd density.

What to Bring

  • Altitude sickness medication
  • Oxygen cylinder
  • Alipay, WeChat Pay
  • Alipay transit card

Final Thoughts

Overall, the pleasant weather was a plus. Despite my initial fears and the lack of information, the experience was fine. The food was good, and the scenery was stunning. I would recommend this trip for people who want to feel the entrance to Tibet or specifically want to visit Qinghai Lake or Chaka Salt Lake. However, if you are not interested in Tibet, Qinghai Lake, or Chaka Salt Lake, this might not be the right destination for you.
