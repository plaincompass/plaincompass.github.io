Before arriving in Datong, I checked Gaode Maps and found what appeared to be a bus route from Datong Airport to the volcano area. I assumed the journey would be straightforward. However, once I arrived, I quickly realized that reaching the bus stop was much harder than I expected.

In the end, I took a taxi to a hotel near the Datong Volcano area. Looking back, I think that was the right decision.

Arriving at Datong Airport

My trip started at Datong Airport.

The airport itself was surprisingly small. Finding the exit was easy, and the terminal felt quiet and uncrowded.

Because Gaode Maps showed a bus route, I expected to take public transportation from the airport to my hotel.

At first everything seemed normal.

There were signs outside the terminal, and the map suggested that I only needed to walk a short distance to reach the bus stop.

Then things became much more confusing.

Why I gave up on the bus

After following the route shown on the map, I found myself standing beside an enormous road.

The more I looked, the less confident I felt.

The road was extremely wide, and I could not find a safe pedestrian crossing. It felt more like infrastructure designed for vehicles than for people on foot.

I asked several local people for help, but everyone seemed to travel by car rather than by bus.

One person suggested continuing farther down the road, but crossing the area did not feel safe to me.

At that point, I decided to stop trying to reach the bus stop and look for a taxi instead.

Taking a taxi to the volcano area

Eventually I booked a taxi.

The ride cost approximately 25 yuan during my visit.

One funny moment happened during the journey.

The driver kept asking whether I was sure about the destination.

He seemed surprised that I wanted to go toward the volcano area rather than toward Datong's more famous attractions.

Using a translation app, I explained that I wanted to see the volcanoes.

His response was essentially:

"There isn't much to see there."

That made me slightly nervous about my choice of destination, but I continued anyway.

Staying at Datong Kaijie Inn (Volcano Park Branch)

After arriving, I checked into Datong Kaijie Inn (Volcano Park Branch).

One reason I booked it was that it appeared to be one of the few options willing to accommodate foreign visitors.

The room itself was reasonably clean and acceptable for a short stay.

However, there was one major problem.

The smoking issue

During my visit, cigarette smoke seemed to be everywhere.

Even after opening the windows, smoke drifted in from:

  • Hallways
  • Other rooms
  • Shared areas
  • Bathroom ventilation

If you are sensitive to cigarette smoke, this is something to keep in mind.

Unexpected extra charge

The room included charging equipment, which initially seemed convenient.

However, I discovered that using it required an additional payment.

Fortunately, I only planned to stay one night before continuing my journey.

Dinner near the hotel

Later that evening, I searched for somewhere to eat.

I chose a local restaurant based mainly on its ratings.

The food was much better than I expected.

I ordered:

  • Rice noodles
  • Cold noodles with peanut sauce

Both dishes were flavorful without being heavily spiced.

I also tried a spiral potato snack.

The taste reminded me of similar street food in Korea, although the price was dramatically lower at only around 4 yuan.

Buying fruit nearby

I also visited a fruit shop near the hotel.

I originally wanted to try local Chinese fruit, but the selection was not what I expected.

Instead, I ended up buying:

  • Jackfruit
  • Japanese grapes

The fruit tasted excellent, but it felt expensive by local standards.

Final thoughts about my first day

My first day in Datong was not really about volcanoes.

It was about figuring out how to reach the volcano area safely and settling in before exploring the region.

The biggest lesson I learned was this:

Do not automatically assume that a route shown on a mapping app will be easy to follow as a pedestrian.

Although public transportation may technically exist, the practical reality can be very different.

For me, taking a taxi from the airport was by far the easiest and safest option.

Despite the transportation challenges, I enjoyed the local food, found a place to stay, and successfully reached the volcano area for the next day's exploration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you get from Datong Airport to the volcano area by public transport?

Possibly, but I found the route confusing and difficult to access safely on foot. I ultimately used a taxi instead.

How much did the taxi cost?

My taxi from the airport to the volcano area cost approximately 25 yuan.

Is Datong Kaijie Inn suitable for foreign visitors?

It appeared to be one of the few accommodation options willing to accept foreign visitors during my trip. Policies can change, so verify before booking.

What was the biggest problem with the hotel?

The most noticeable problem was cigarette smoke throughout the building, including common areas and nearby rooms.

Was staying near the volcano area worth it?

For my trip it was worthwhile because I planned to explore the volcano area the following morning. The location was much more convenient than staying in central Datong.

Would you recommend taking a taxi instead of the bus?

Based on my experience, yes. The taxi was inexpensive and much easier than attempting to navigate the airport bus route as a first-time visitor.
