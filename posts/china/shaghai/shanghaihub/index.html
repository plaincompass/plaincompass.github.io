Shanghai Travel Hub

Location: Shanghai

Region Overview

Shanghai includes Shanghai Disneyland, Wukang Mansion, and Zhujiajiao.

Shanghai Disneyland is a theme park where visitors can experience Zootopia Village. Wukang Mansion is a European-style building located in Changning District. Zhujiajiao is a traditional water village situated in Qingpu District, described as the nearest such village to Shanghai’s city center.

Places Covered

Shanghai Disneyland

Shanghai Disneyland is a theme park featuring Zootopia Village.

Visitors can check attraction wait times using the Shanghai Disney app. The park is accessible by subway and on foot. Crowds were moderate, and lines were shorter than those experienced in Orlando. Disney crew members managed the queues. Visitors received a Mickey Mouse sticker upon entry. The buildings and signs in Zootopia Village are lit up at night.

Wukang Mansion

Wukang Mansion is a European-style building in Changning District, Shanghai, which served as a filming location for the movie Sekgae.

The area surrounding the mansion contains bakeries and Northeast-style restaurants. The building is visible from Home Inn Hotel, with a 30-second walk from the room to the façade. The hotel is accessible from Shanghai Sookbong Station by subway. Payment at the hotel was made with Alipay or WeChat Pay. Staff used a translator machine. Crowds are large, with early morning hours between 6 and 7 AM noted as a time before crowds arrive.

Zhujiajiao

Zhujiajiao is a traditional water village in Qingpu District.

The village features narrow streets, an arch bridge, and a swing-shaped bench under a purple acacia tree. The location is accessible by subway. Paths are flat. The weather was clear and not humid. Crowds were lighter than expected.
