Xining Travel Hub

Location: Xining

Region Overview

Xining is the location for Ta'ershan and Chaka Salt Lake. Xining is situated in Qinghai, China, and serves as the departure point for trips to these sites.

Ta'ershan is accessible from Xining via Bus No. 909 from the Xining Bus Terminal. Chaka Salt Lake is reachable from Xining via a direct tourist train. Both locations are situated in Qinghai Province.

Places Covered

Ta'ershan

Ta'ershan is a location at 2,600m altitude characterized by 7 stone stupas at the entrance.

The site features a 20-minute uphill walk from the bus stop to the entrance. Bus No. 909 from the Xining Bus Terminal costs 4 yuan per person. The admission fee is 70 yuan. The total time required for the trip, including travel and on-site stay, was approximately 5 hours.

Chaka Salt Lake

Chaka Salt Lake is a high-altitude lake near Qinghai Lake, often called China's "Mirror of the Sky."

Visitors can take a direct tourist train from Xining, which takes approximately five hours. The train includes sleeper berths. The location involves high altitude.

Transportation Summary

A taxi transfer from the airport to Xining Station cost 120 yuan. Alipay and WeChat Pay are used for payment. Bus No. 909 operates from the Xining Bus Terminal to Ta'ershan for 4 yuan per person. A direct tourist train operates from Xining to Chaka Salt Lake, taking approximately five hours, and includes sleeper berths.
