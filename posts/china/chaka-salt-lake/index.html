Is Chaka Salt Lake Worth Visiting? My Experience Taking the Daytime Sleeper Train from Xining

Chaka Salt Lake is often called China's "Mirror of the Sky." Like many travelers, I was drawn by photographs showing endless reflections where the sky and ground seem to merge into one. In August 2025, I finally made the trip to see whether Chaka Salt Lake was truly worth the journey.

While the landscape is undeniably beautiful, visitors should be prepared for crowds, high altitude, and a long journey from Xining. Here is my experience visiting Chaka Salt Lake, including transportation, photography recommendations, and practical travel tips.

Getting There: A Comfortable Five-Hour Train Journey

One of the highlights of the entire trip was the train ride itself.

I traveled from Xining to Chaka by direct tourist train. The journey took approximately five hours and was much more comfortable than I expected because the train included sleeper berths despite operating during the daytime.

Unlike standard trains with upright seats, passengers can relax in sleeper compartments while enjoying the scenery across Qinghai Province. For me, the train journey was not just transportation. It became an enjoyable part of the overall travel experience.

The train is not the only option, but it is by far the best one. You can also take an express bus or tourist bus between Xining and Chaka (which you can find online and book through Trip.com). However, the buses take more than six hours and the ride is quite rough. I took the train from Xining to Chaka and an express bus on the return trip to Xining, and I definitely recommend taking the train.

The main drawback of the train is its limited schedule, as there is usually only one direct train each day. While buses provide an alternative, missing the train means facing a much longer and rougher bus journey. If you intend to travel independently, it is worth organizing your train tickets well in advance.

First Impressions: Beautiful but Extremely Crowded

The scenery at Chaka Salt Lake is impressive.

When the conditions are right, the shallow water creates beautiful reflections that make the landscape feel almost unreal. During the daytime, the reflected sky creates the famous mirror effect that attracts photographers from across China.

Unfortunately, the destination is also extremely popular.

When I visited in August, the main scenic area was packed with visitors. Taking photos without strangers appearing in the frame was often difficult, especially around the most famous viewpoints.

Anyone looking for complete solitude may be disappointed by the number of tourists during peak season.

Why July and August Are Actually Worth Considering

Many travelers hesitate to visit during July and August because it is peak travel season and crowds can be significant.

However, there is also a major advantage.

July and August are part of the rainy season in Qinghai, which means there is usually more water covering the surface of the salt flats. This additional water is what helps create the spectacular mirror reflections that Chaka Salt Lake is famous for.

When the weather cooperates, the sky reflects beautifully across the shallow water, creating scenes that look almost unreal. In my opinion, the improved reflection conditions make the summer crowds more tolerable, especially if photography is one of your main reasons for visiting.

My Favorite Photography Spot: Sky One Scenic Area

The best discovery of the trip was Sky One Scenic Area.

Most visitors spend their time in the original and more famous section of Chaka Salt Lake. However, after exploring that area, I took a taxi to Sky One Scenic Area and immediately noticed the difference. The ride took about 15 minutes and cost around 55 yuan. As a practical tip, catching a cab from the hotel can be difficult, so be sure to get your driver's phone number when you head to the hotel.

The area felt more spacious, significantly less crowded, and much better suited for photography.

One of the most memorable features was the collection of salt sculptures spread throughout the area. Combined with the open landscape and smaller crowds, they created many photography opportunities that were difficult to find in the main scenic zone.

Because there were fewer people around, it was much easier to take clean landscape photographs without waiting for crowds to move away.

If photography is one of the main reasons for visiting Chaka Salt Lake, I strongly recommend making time for Sky One Scenic Area.

Guests staying at Chaka Sky One Hotel may also find accommodation packages that include scenic area admission and allow entry before regular opening hours. For photographers, this can be a major advantage.

Stay Overnight If Photography Matters

If you are visiting Chaka Salt Lake primarily for photography, I strongly recommend staying at least one night nearby.

The reason is simple: wind.

The famous mirror effect depends on calm water. When strong winds arrive, the water surface becomes disturbed and the reflections can disappear completely.

During my visit, it became obvious how much weather conditions influence the final photos. Even if the sky is clear, wind alone can significantly reduce reflection quality.

An overnight stay gives you an additional opportunity to return when conditions improve. For photographers, this flexibility can make the difference between ordinary photos and the iconic mirror-like images that make Chaka Salt Lake famous.

Altitude and Things to Prepare

Chaka Salt Lake is located at high altitude, so preparation is important.

The terrain itself is not physically demanding, but some travelers may feel the effects of the thinner air.

I would recommend bringing:

  • Portable oxygen canisters
  • Altitude sickness medication
  • Sunglasses
  • Sunscreen
  • Plenty of drinking water

Anyone with previous altitude-related issues should prepare carefully before visiting.

What I Liked Most

The most memorable part of the trip was the daytime sleeper train journey from Xining.

Being able to lie down comfortably during the five-hour journey made the transportation experience surprisingly enjoyable.

I also loved the scenery.

During the day, the reflected sky creates beautiful mirror-like views across the salt flats. Although I did not stay overnight, you can still experience the night sky around 9:00 PM, near closing time, when countless stars become visible above the plateau. It gets quite dark around that time, so if you plan to walk around, make sure to bring a flashlight or use your phone's flash.

The contrast between the bright daytime reflections and the star-filled evening sky made the destination feel completely different depending on the time of day.

Sky One Scenic Area was another highlight. Compared with the main scenic area, it felt much less crowded and provided significantly better conditions for photography.

What I Liked Least

The biggest downside was transportation flexibility and comfort when traveling by bus.

With only one direct train operating per day, rail options are limited. While express and tourist buses are available via Trip.com, the bus journey takes over six hours and is quite rough compared to the train.

The second drawback was the crowds.

The scenery is undeniably beautiful, but the number of visitors in the main scenic area can make the experience feel more commercialized than many people expect.

Who Should Visit?

I would recommend Chaka Salt Lake to:

  • Photography enthusiasts
  • Landscape lovers
  • Travelers interested in unique natural scenery
  • People who want a salt-flat experience but find Bolivia's Salar de Uyuni too far away

I would be less likely to recommend it to:

  • Travelers who have already experienced Salar de Uyuni
  • People who strongly dislike crowds
  • Travelers with significant altitude-related health concerns

Final Thoughts

Although reaching Chaka Salt Lake requires a long journey, traveling there from Xining is relatively straightforward once transportation is arranged.

The five-hour daytime sleeper train was one of the most enjoyable parts of the experience, while the reflections across the salt flats created some of the most memorable scenery I have seen in China.

For photographers, I highly recommend visiting Sky One Scenic Area rather than spending all of your time in the main tourist zone.

I also recommend staying at least one night near the lake. Wind conditions have a huge impact on photography, and having an extra day dramatically increases your chances of experiencing the famous mirror effect under ideal conditions.

Despite the crowds, Chaka Salt Lake remains one of the most unique landscapes I have visited.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get to Chaka Salt Lake from Xining?

The most convenient option is the direct tourist train between Xining and Chaka. The journey takes approximately five hours and uses sleeper berths despite operating during the daytime. There is usually only one direct train per day, so advance planning is recommended. Express buses and tourist buses (bookable via Trip.com) are also available, though they take over six hours and the ride is rougher.

Is Chaka Salt Lake crowded?

Yes. During peak seasons, especially July and August, the main scenic area can become extremely crowded. Visitors looking for fewer people may prefer Sky One Scenic Area.

Why are July and August good months to visit Chaka Salt Lake?

Although these months are part of the busiest travel season, they are also part of the rainy season. The additional water on the salt flats improves the chances of seeing the famous mirror reflections.

What is the best photography spot at Chaka Salt Lake?

My favorite location was Sky One Scenic Area. It was noticeably less crowded than the main scenic zone and offered better photography opportunities and interesting salt sculptures.

Should I stay overnight at Chaka Salt Lake?

Yes. I strongly recommend staying at least one night. Wind conditions can dramatically affect the lake's reflections, and an extra day gives you another chance to photograph the famous mirror effect under better conditions.

Do I need altitude sickness medication for Chaka Salt Lake?

Travelers sensitive to altitude should consider bringing altitude sickness medication and portable oxygen canisters. Preparing in advance can make the experience much more comfortable.

Is Chaka Salt Lake worth visiting?

If you enjoy photography, unusual landscapes, and independent travel, Chaka Salt Lake is worth visiting. However, visitors should be prepared for crowds and a long journey from Xining.
