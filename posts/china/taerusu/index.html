Ta'ershan Monastery: Monks' Chants and a 2,600m Altitude Walk

It takes 20 minutes to climb the hill to reach Ta'ershan at 2,600m altitude. It is characterized by monks' chants and 7 stone stupas at the entrance.

In August 2025, while staying at the Qinghai Xiadu Hotel (Qinghai Xiadu Hotel) in Xining, I took a day trip from Xining to Ta'ershan. I traveled by taking Bus No. 909 from the Xining Bus Terminal, which is an intuitive public transport option with a reasonable cost of 4 yuan per person. The total time required, including travel time and on-site stay, was approximately 5 hours.

This experience was significantly influenced by the high altitude of 2,600m. The 20-minute uphill walk from the bus stop to the entrance was particularly tiring. Nevertheless, thanks to the spacious grounds, the unique Tibetan Buddhist atmosphere, and the 7 circular stone stupas at the entrance, I felt the 70 yuan admission fee was justified. It will be a worthwhile visit for those interested in exotic cultures who can handle a walk and potential altitude sickness.

Reason for Visit

In August 2025, I was staying in the Xining area of Qinghai Province, and my main destination was Chaka Salt Lake. However, I decided to visit Ta'ershan, which is accessible by city bus from a hotel near Xining Station. The total time required, including travel time, was approximately 5 hours.

Impressive Points

Experiencing Tibetan Buddhist culture was a first for me, and the atmosphere was impressive. The guide signs installed throughout the site were mainly in Tibetan, looking as if the characters were dancing. I watched monks singing chants to music and burning incense; their voices blended together like instruments and echoed. I could also see a monk blowing a trumpet from the roof at set times. Additionally, I witnessed a person praying by bowing 3 times and clapping once for their father's soul.

The site was lively with many people. While taking the bus, I saw a large family dressed in traditional Tibetan clothing riding together, which added to the exotic atmosphere.

The memory that remained particularly strong for me was the 7 circular stone stupas right in front of the entrance. They were excellent for photo backgrounds. Overall, the grounds were very spacious, so the admission fee was not a waste despite the cost. The Tibetan languages written on the signs also became exotic photo backgrounds.

Practical Information

Transportation I traveled using Bus No. 909 from the Xining Bus Terminal. The fare is 4 yuan per person. The bus route is somewhat long, taking about 1 hour and 20 minutes one way, but it is an intuitive public transport option.

Physical Fitness and Altitude This area is located at a high altitude of 2,600m. After getting off the bus, you must cross a hill to arrive at Ta'ershan. This uphill walk takes about 20 minutes and was more tiring than usual due to the altitude. If you are susceptible to altitude sickness, it is recommended to use a taxi or bring oxygen and medication. The round-trip walking time was approximately 3 hours.

Admission and Cost The admission fee is approximately 70 yuan, which was more expensive than expected. I waited about 4 minutes to buy a ticket upon first entry. Foreigners can pass by presenting their passport and were guided to a side entrance for slightly faster entry.

Language and Payment I could not speak Chinese, so communication was sometimes difficult. However, when I told the bus driver I wanted to go to Ta'ershan, he understood. I used a translation app for shopping, which was sufficient. Alipay and WeChat Pay can be used everywhere, and cash is rarely needed in China. More places accepted WeChat Pay than Alipay.

Crowd Levels and Time of Day I visited between 2 PM and 4 PM. There were many people, but the flow was manageable.

Final Thoughts

It was almost as expected, but the physical burden of walking from the bus stop due to the altitude was greater than anticipated. It will be a worthwhile visit for those interested in Tibetan culture or wanting to experience the exotic atmosphere of Tibet. It may not be suitable for those who have difficulty walking due to altitude sickness or those who are not interested in Buddhism.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I get from Xining to Ta'ershan?

You can take Bus No. 909 from the Xining Bus Terminal. The fare is 4 yuan per person.

How long does a trip to Ta'ershan take, and what is the physical effort?

The total trip time is approximately 5 hours. If doing a day trip, it is good to expect a half-day. After getting off the bus, you must climb a hill for about 20 minutes to reach the site, which can be tiring due to the 2,600m altitude. The round-trip walking time is approximately 2 hours and 30 minutes.

What is the admission fee for Ta'ershan?

The admission fee is approximately 70 yuan. It is said to be 40 yuan during the off-season, based on peak season rates.

What payment methods are accepted in the Ta'ershan area?

Alipay and WeChat Pay can be used everywhere, with more places accepting WeChat Pay than Alipay. Cash is not needed.

Is Ta'ershan suitable for people susceptible to altitude sickness?

It may not be suitable for those who have difficulty walking due to altitude sickness. If you are susceptible to altitude sickness, it is recommended to use a taxi or bring oxygen and medication.

What were the crowd levels at Ta'ershan?

The atmosphere was lively with many people. Even when I visited between 2 PM and 4 PM, there were many people, but the flow was manageable.

How do foreigners enter Ta'ershan?

Foreigners can pass by presenting their passport and were guided to a side entrance for slightly faster entry.
