Zhanqiao Pier: Fog, Seagulls, and Crowds in Qingdao

Walked Zhanqiao Pier in thick fog, overwhelmed by seagulls and TikTok crowds. Tried China-only McMornings but found the chaos exhausting.

I visited Zhanqiao Pier in February 2025, drawn by its reputation as a landmark in Qingdao. I walked from my accommodation—just a five‑minute stroll—and spent about 20 minutes on the path that stretches into the sea.

The day was crowded and foggy, making visibility low and the scene bustling with people filming on TikTok. Seagulls dominated the scene, with many feeding on the ground and flocks adding to the chaos. Despite the local McMornings at a nearby McDonald’s, the overall experience felt more hectic than enjoyable, and I decided not to return.

Why I Visited

I had heard that Zhanqiao Pier was famous for its building extending over the sea, and I wanted to see that impressive sight for myself. The pier’s reputation drew me in, especially since it’s a well‑known spot in Qingdao.

What Stood Out

The main attraction was the path that stretches out into the sea. I walked along it for about 20 minutes, which felt like a quick stroll from my accommodation—just five minutes on foot. Along the way, I stopped at a nearby McDonald’s and tried the McMornings that are sold only in China, which was a fun local treat.
However, the experience was heavily dominated by seagulls. There were a lot of them on the path, and people were feeding them, which made the situation even more chaotic. The fog was thick, so visibility was poor, and it was difficult to walk all the way while filming on TikTok, especially with the seagulls and TikTok filers around. The seagull flocks and the TikTok filers left the biggest impression on me.

Practical Notes

  • Timing: I visited at 9 a.m. in February 2025.
  • Crowds: It was very, very crowded, more so than I expected.
  • Weather: Cloudy with thick fog, reducing visibility.
  • Transportation: I walked from my accommodation; no other transport was needed.
  • Payment: I used Alipay/WeChat Pay.
  • Fitness: No special fitness required, but the seagulls and the crowd of TikTok filers made it tiring.
  • Language: A grandmother who sells seagull food approached me, but I couldn’t understand her.
  • Photo spots: It was hard to find a good spot because seagulls and people blocked everything.

Final Thoughts

Overall, the pier was chaotic due to the crowds and seagull flocks. The fame of the place attracted too many people, which made the visit less enjoyable than I had hoped. I have no intention of revisiting.
