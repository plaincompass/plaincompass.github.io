Badaguan, Qingdao: Rainy Morning Stroll Through European Villas

A quiet, rainy morning walk through Badaguan's historic European-style villas. With almost no crowds, the flat streets and sea views made for a peaceful one-hour visit.

Badaguan, Qingdao

I visited Badaguan in Qingdao in February 2025 to explore its historic European-style villas by the sea. I arrived at 8:00 AM, just as most attractions were still closed, and the quiet streets were nearly empty despite the rain. The peaceful atmosphere made for an undisturbed morning walk.

The area is flat and easy to explore on foot, although I ended up walking more than expected because Badaguan covers a fairly large area. I traveled from May Fourth Square by subway, which took about 20 minutes, and paid using Alipay and WeChat Pay. Even though my visit lasted only about an hour, it was enough to appreciate the district's unique architectural character and seaside setting.

Why I Visited

I visited Badaguan because I wanted to see Qingdao's historic European-style architecture. The area is famous for its tree-lined streets, old villas, and coastal scenery, making it one of the city's most distinctive neighborhoods.

What Stood Out

The quiet atmosphere was the highlight of my visit. Because of the rain and early hour, there were almost no tourists around. The villas looked especially photogenic against the seaside backdrop, and the broad streets made the area pleasant to explore on foot.

Although the terrain was flat and easy to walk, the district was larger than I expected, so covering multiple streets required more walking than I had planned during my short visit.

Practical Notes

  • Transport: I took the subway from the area around May Fourth Square. The journey took roughly 20 minutes one way.
  • Timing: I arrived at 8:00 AM, and most attractions were still closed. Arriving after 10:00 AM would provide access to more buildings and museums.
  • Weather: It rained throughout my visit, but walking was still comfortable.
  • Crowds: Very few visitors were present during the morning.
  • Payment: Alipay and WeChat Pay worked without issue.
  • Fitness: The terrain is mostly flat and suitable for casual walking.
  • Duration: My visit lasted about one hour.

Final Thoughts

Badaguan felt like a peaceful seaside district filled with historic European-style villas. During the early morning, most of the people I encountered were locals out for a walk or exercise. I had hoped to visit Huashi House and several other historic buildings, but they were not yet open when I arrived.

Compared with Hongshan Road, Badaguan feels more spacious and elegant, although I personally prefer the atmosphere of Hongshan Road. If you enjoy architecture, quiet walks, and sea views, Badaguan is well worth visiting, especially on a calm morning after the rain.
