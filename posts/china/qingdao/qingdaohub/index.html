Qingdao Travel Hub

Location: Qingdao

Region Overview

Qingdao includes Badaguan, the area around Hengshan Road in the old town, Signal Mountain Park, and Zhanqiao Pier.

Badaguan is located in the coastal area of Qingdao, while Hengshan Road is situated in the historic German architecture district of the old town. Signal Mountain Park overlooks the historic German-style village in Qingdao, and Zhanqiao Pier is a landmark extending into the sea near the old town.

Places Covered

Badaguan

Badaguan is a district in Qingdao known for its historic European-style villas, tree-lined streets, and coastal scenery.

The area is flat and easy to explore on foot, though it covers a large area. A visitor walked through the streets during a rainy morning. The location is accessible via subway from May Fourth Square, a trip that took about 20 minutes. Payment for the subway was made using Alipay and WeChat Pay.

Hengshan Road (Qingdao Old Town)

Hengshan Road is a street in Shinan District, Qingdao, characterized by historic German architecture and a European-style atmosphere.

A visitor stayed at the Man Xi Hotel on this road. The hotel is about a 20-minute walk from Zhanqiao Pier. Public transportation options in the immediate area are limited. A visitor walked to a nearby market and received a foot massage in the neighborhood.

Signal Mountain Park

Signal Mountain Park is a park in Qingdao that offers a panoramic vantage point over the historic German-style village.

The park features a steep but well-maintained path to the summit. A visitor hiked the park starting at 8 a.m. The walk from Man Xi Pub to the park entrance took approximately 10 minutes. A café is located inside a tall red building within the park.

Zhanqiao Pier

Zhanqiao Pier is a landmark in Qingdao featuring a path that stretches out into the sea.

A visitor walked along the pier for about 20 minutes. The location is a five-minute walk from the accommodation on Hengshan Road. A McDonald's is located nearby, where China-only McMornings were sold. Seagulls were present on the path, and people were observed feeding them.
