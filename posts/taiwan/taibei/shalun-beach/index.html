Shalun Beach Sunset: Humid June Escape from Taipei

Subway trip to Shalun Beach for a yellow sunset. Wet ground reflected light, making photos stunning despite the humid June heat.

Shalun Beach, New Taipei City

I was originally planning to visit Danshui, but after searching for a nearby alternative, I discovered Shalun Beach, a spot that locals frequently visit. Departing from the Sky 19 Hotel near Taipei Station in June 2024, we took a quick subway ride and arrived around 6 PM. As the sun dipped below the horizon, the sky turned a beautiful yellow, creating a stunning backdrop for the beach.

The beach itself was wonderful, with slightly wet ground that reflected the sunset light, making every photo a standout. While the hot and humid June air in Taipei made us tire quickly, the scenery was beautiful enough to offset the discomfort.

Trip Overview

My initial plan was to go to Danshui, but I found Shalun Beech through a search for a special place frequented by locals. Staying at the Sky 19 Hotel near Taipei Station, the trip was a convenient subway ride from my accommodation.

Highlights

I arrived at Shalun Beach around 6 PM, right as the sun was setting. The sky turned a beautiful yellow during the tram-like train ride to the beach. Although Taipei was still hot, the temperature at sunset was slightly lower than during the day, allowing me to enjoy the evening without the scorching sun.

The scenery at Shalun Beach was wonderful, and the views on the way were equally beautiful. There were many locals present, along with a few people taking wedding photos or having picnics, though the heat made picnics less appealing. The ground was slightly wet at sunset, reflecting the light and enhancing the visual appeal of the location.

Practical Notes

  • Transport: Subway from the main Taipei accommodation (Sky 19 Hotel near Taipei Station).
  • Navigation: The specific tagging machine or landmark is hard to find when getting off near the beach station.
  • Crowd: A few locals were around; it was not crowded.
  • Duration: The whole trip took about 1 hour.
  • Fitness: The train ride was cool, but the June heat in Taipei makes you tire quickly.
  • Weather: Very clear, but humid and hot.
  • Language & Payment: No major language barrier; many places accepted card payment.
  • What to bring: Handheld fan, water, hand towels, mosquito repellent.

Final Thoughts

It was humid and hot, but Shalun Beach was incredibly beautiful. The slightly wet ground at sunset reflected the light, making every photo a highlight. While picnics are not recommended in this heat, visiting during a cooler season would make Shalun Beach a great spot for a relaxed outing away from downtown Taipei.
