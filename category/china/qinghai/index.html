Ta'ershan, Qinghai Province, China

Ta'ershan Monastery: Monks' Chants and a 2,600m Altitude Walk

It takes 20 minutes to climb the hill to reach Ta'ershan at 2,600m altitude. It is characterized by monks' chants and 7 stone stupas at the entrance.
